Conventions of this catalogue

Monetary entries are filed in thousands of United States dollars and printed at full magnitude. Probabilities below one thousandth of a percent are set in exponential form rather than rounded away; an exact zero is recorded as 0 and plotted at the log-axis floor. RS is carried as 100 · TiC — the TiC scale disagrees with the worked example in the course deck, so RS is treated as the practical scale throughout.
